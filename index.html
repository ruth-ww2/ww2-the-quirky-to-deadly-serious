About the Author

Ruth Sabath Rosenthal

A New York City poet, well published in the U.S. and internationally. Writing since 2000 — and tutored by well-regarded New York poets, primarily at the 92nd Street Y — her work spans five poetry collections, a myriad of journals and anthologies, and a 2006 Pushcart Prize nomination. She also edited the memoir Manfred.
